Core Research Pillars

Bridging synthetic organic chemistry, plasmonic nanomaterials, and chemical biology to develop next-generation diagnostic and therapeutic tools.

🧬
Molecular Probes

Enzyme-Selective FAP Diagnostics

Designed enzyme-selective nanoparticle-based fluorescent probes targeting Fibroblast Activation Protein (FAP) in tumor microenvironments, achieving high selectivity discrimination over DPP-4 and PREP.

FAP Probes DPP-4 / PREP Peptide SAR Fluorescence

Nanomedicine

Functionalized Gold Nanorods (AuNR)

Developed smart plasmonic platforms and layer-by-layer films containing gold nanorods and nanoparticles for sequential drug release, photothermal activation, and enhanced combination cancer therapy.

Au Nanorods PEGylation Drug Delivery Photothermal

Bio-Evaluation & Cellular Imaging
