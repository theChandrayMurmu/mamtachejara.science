Research Profile

Organic chemistry researcher with a Ph.D. in Convergence Science from Sahmyook University, South Korea, and an M.Sc. in Organic Chemistry from Fergusson College, India. Research experience focused on enzyme-selective nanoparticle-based probes for cancer-related applications, particularly fibroblast activation protein (FAP) targeting. Experienced in functionalized gold nanomaterials, fluorescent molecular probes, PEGylation strategies, enzyme-selectivity studies, fluorescence-based assays, cellular evaluation, and nanomaterial characterization.
